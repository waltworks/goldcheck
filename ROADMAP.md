## Design Principles

1. Keep GoldCheck lightweight and easy to use.
2. Prioritize gold jewelry buying and selling.
3. Use one shared metal-value calculation engine.
4. Avoid unnecessary graphs and complicated financial tools.
5. Clearly label calculated values as estimates.
6. Preserve original purchase records and historical prices.
7. Keep calculations functional offline.
8. Allow users to back up their collection data.
